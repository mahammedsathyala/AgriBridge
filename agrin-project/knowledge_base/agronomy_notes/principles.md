# AgriN Knowledge Base: Agronomy & Regenerative Agriculture Guidelines

This document provides agronomic reference guidelines, regional crop suitability matrices, and ecological regeneration practices for smallholder farmers within the BRICS agricultural cooperation framework.

## Core Regenerative Agriculture Principles

1. **Continuous Living Roots**: Cultivate crops with varied root architectures (deep taproots, dense fibrous roots) throughout all seasons to build liquid carbon pathways and sustain soil microbial ecosystems.
2. **Soil Armor (Mulching & Ground Cover)**: Keep topsoil protected from direct solar insolation and rainfall kinetic energy using crop residue or low-growing companion legumes.
3. **Biological Diversity**: Intercropping, agroforestry borders, and crop rotations (cereals + nitrogen-fixing pulses + brassica bio-fumigants) break pest cycles and reduce reliance on synthetic chemical inputs.
4. **Minimal Soil Disturbance**: Shift away from deep inversion tilling toward no-till or minimum tillage to preserve soil organic matter (SOM) and arbuscular mycorrhizal fungi (AMF).
5. **Livestock & Organic Integration**: Integrate farmyard manure, vermicompost, and biochar to accelerate humus formation.

## Regional Agro-Ecological Focus

- **Semi-Arid Tropics (India, Southern Africa, Northeast Brazil)**: Millets (Bajra, Ragi, Jowar), Pigeon Pea, Cowpea, drought-resilient legumes.
- **Sub-Tropical / Temperate Black & Clay Soils (Central India, Russia, Brazil)**: Chickpea, Lentil, Sorghum, Soybean-intercropped cereals, bio-fumigant mustard.
- **River Basin Alluvial Soils (China, Indo-Gangetic Plains)**: Short-duration catch crops (Green Gram), Sesbania green manuring, zero-till wheat systems.
